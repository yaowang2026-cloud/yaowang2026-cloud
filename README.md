### 📌 Featured Project

#### 🛍️ [Semantic Product Recommendations with PySpark](https://github.com/yaowang2026-cloud/Machine-Learning-with-PySpark-Recommender-System)

Built a content-based recommendation prototype that connects product descriptions with recent browsing activity to support retail product discovery.

- **Semantic features:** Encoded product titles and descriptions using OpenAI’s `text-embedding-3-small` model with 512-dimensional embeddings.
- **Catalog modeling:** Applied PySpark K-means to organize the 2,000 products shown in the saved visualization into five clusters; used PCA and Plotly to explore their structure.
- **Recommendation workflow:** Identified clusters associated with eight recently viewed products, excluded viewed items, and sampled five unseen products from each selected cluster.
- **Saved demonstration:** Generated 10 recommendations spanning rugs and paints. Selection is random within clusters; relevance ranking and quantitative evaluation are future improvements.

**Tech stack:** Python · PySpark · OpenAI Embeddings · pandas · NumPy · Plotly
